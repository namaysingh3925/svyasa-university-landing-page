S-VYASA Deemed to be University

Welcome to the world of S-VYASA, which stands for Swami Vivekananda Yoga Anusandhana Samsthana. It is a unique university dedicated to the study and promotion of yoga and its applications, based on the teachings of Swami Vivekananda.



Located in Bengaluru, India, S-VYASA has pioneered making yoga a socially relevant science. The university's mission is to combine the best of the East (traditional yoga and spiritual lore) with the best of the West (modern scientific research) to produce a new generation of yoga practitioners and researchers.
